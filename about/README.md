## 📖 About

My journey into technology has been purposeful rather than conventional. From a young age I was captivated by computers and video games, spending countless hours exploring them with wonder. This early curiosity first drew me toward video game development, but over time I realised my passion extended to technology as a whole. I often imagined what it might be like to communicate with computers more directly and to be part of the wider world of technology, but felt it was out of reach due to doubts about my abilities.

In order to grow my abilities, I decided to first build a foundation, and worked in a restaurant, which instilled hard work, resilience, and discipline. Determined to refine myself further, I completed an Access course in college, a degree in Psychology, and worked across healthcare, events hospitality, and cultural heritage. Though my degree and experience strengthened my communication and adaptability, it also broadened my perspective, deepened my understanding of people and systems, and helped me overcome those doubts. I am now able to put forth my best efforts into finding my path in technology, with what was once wonder becoming a craft, and what began as curiosity a deliberate pursuit – a reminder that they were not just early sparks, but enduring threads in my work.

This portfolio is now woven into my journey and integral to my current path. It is not a definitive statement or a showcase of perfection, but a living record of learning, persistence, consistency, and dedication, capturing both the progress I have made and the areas that are still evolving. Each project reflects not only technical practice but also a broader engagement with technology, from design and problem solving to communication and intelligent systems, showing how different disciplines and perspectives come together in my work. I endeavour to create a portfolio that is impactful, transparent, and intentional, so that it not only demonstrates what I have built, but also the thinking, influences, and reasoning that shape it.

In terms of the values embedded in this portfolio, I strive to work ethically with: integrity by being honest about the process, authenticity by building only what I genuinely believe in, empathy by taking accountability for how my work affects those who engage with it, and humility by remembering there is always more to learn. These values guide not only my work in this portfolio, but also how my work contributes more broadly and how I hope to work with others. They serve as my North Star in any situation, be it when facing challenges or making decisions, keeping me aligned with the principles that matter most.

Technology gives us so many ways to grow, connect, and create, and when guided by kindness, I believe it is an invaluable tool that can encourage and empower us in all of our journeys. With it being a shared language, it also allows versatile ideas to travel, evolve, and spark new possibilities. So whether you are a fellow contributor, a potential collaborator, someone seeking inspiration, or simply passing through, thank you for taking the time to explore – it truly is appreciated. I hope that something has resonated with you, and I sincerely wish you success and fulfilment in your own journey.
