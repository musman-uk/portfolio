## 📖 About  

My journey into technology has been anything but conventional. From a young age I was captivated by computers and video games, spending countless hours exploring them with wonder. That early curiosity first drew me toward game development, but over time I realised my passion extended to technology as a whole. I often imagined what it might be like to communicate with computers more directly and to be part of the wider world of technology, but felt it was out of reach due to doubts about my abilities.  

In order to grow my abilities, I decided to first build a foundation and worked in a restaurant, where I learnt the value of responsibility, hard work, and discipline. Determined to refine myself further, I completed an access course in college and then a degree in Psychology. I then went on to work across sectors including healthcare, events, hospitality, and cultural heritage. My degree and work experience broadened my perspective, deepened my understanding of people and systems, and helped me overcome those doubts about my abilities. With this background, and my Mum's encouragement, I was able to trust my abilities and put forth my best efforts into finding my path in technology – what once had felt like wonder is becoming a craft, and what began as curiosity is growing into a deliberate pursuit.  

This portfolio is woven into my journey and my current path in technology. It is not a definitive statement or a showcase of perfection, but a living record of learning, persistence, and dedication. Each project reflects not only technical practice but also a broader engagement with technology, from design and problem solving to communication and intelligent systems. I endeavour to create work that is consistent, transparent, and reproducible, with demonstrations paired directly with source material so that it remains accessible and verifiable. In this way, my portfolio is not only a reflection of what I have built, but also of how I think, learn, and approach challenges.  

When it comes to how I work, I value clarity, maintainability, and authenticity. I document outcomes as well as blockers and insights, because the process is as meaningful as the result. I place importance on integrity, by being honest and transparent about the process, and on empathy, by considering how my work could affect those who engage with it.  

Technology gives us so many ways to grow, to connect, and to create. I believe it is an invaluable tool that can encourage and empower us in all of our journeys. Whether you are a fellow contributor, a potential collaborator, someone seeking inspiration, or simply passing through, thank you for taking the time to explore – it is truly appreciated, and I sincerely wish you success and fulfilment in your own journey.
