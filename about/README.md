## 📖 About

My journey into technology has been anything but conventional. From a young age I was captivated by computers and video games, spending countless hours exploring them with wonder. This early curiosity first drew me toward video game development, but over time I realised my passion extended to technology as a whole. I often imagined what it might be like to communicate with computers more directly and to be part of the wider world of technology, but felt it was out of reach due to doubts about my abilities.

In order to grow my abilities, I decided to first build a foundation, and worked in a restaurant, which instilled responsibility, hard work, resilience, patience, and discipline. Determined to refine myself further, I completed an access course in college, a degree in Psychology, and worked across healthcare, events hospitality, and cultural heritage. Though my degree and experience strengthened my capability for clear communication and adaptability, it also broadened my perspective, deepened my understanding of people and systems, and helped me overcome those doubts about my abilities. With this background, and my Mum's encouragement, I was able to trust my abilities and put forth my best efforts into finding my path in technology – what once had felt like wonder is becoming a craft, and what began as curiosity is growing into a deliberate pursuit.

This portfolio is now woven into my journey and integral to my current path in technology. It is not a definitive statement or a showcase of perfection, but a living record of learning, persistence, consistency, and dedication, capturing both the progress I have made and the areas where I continue to grow. Each project reflects not only technical practice but also a broader engagement with technology, from design and problem solving to communication and intelligent systems, showing how different disciplines and perspectives come together in my work. I endeavour to create a portfolio that is impactful, transparent, and intentional, so that it not only demonstrates what I have built, but also the thinking, influences, and reasoning that shape it.

In terms of the values embedded in this portfolio, I strive to work with integrity by being honest about the process, with authenticity by building only what I genuinely believe in, with empathy by considering how my work may affect those who engage with it, and with humility by remembering there is always more to learn. These values guide not only my work in this portfolio, but also how my work contributes more broadly and how I hope to work with others. They serve as a reminder when facing challenges or making decisions, keeping me aligned with the principles that matter most.

Technology gives us so many ways to grow, to connect, and to create, I believe it is an invaluable tool that can encourage and empower us in all of our journeys. It is also a shared language, one that allows ideas to travel, evolve, and spark new possibilities across boundaries. So whether you are a fellow contributor, a potential collaborator, someone seeking inspiration, or simply passing through, thank you for taking the time to explore – it truly is appreciated, and I sincerely wish you success and fulfilment in your own journey.
