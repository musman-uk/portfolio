## 📖 About  

My journey in technology began as curiosity with computers and video games at an early age. It has since grown into
a deeper passion and the work I do, because I believe when technology is truthful, responsible, and kind, 
it can do something good.

For me to work in a way that could do some good, I strive to have integrity
by being honest about the process, authenticity by building only what I genuinely believe in, empathy 
by being accountable for how my work can affect others, and humility by remembering 
there is always more to learn.

So whether you are a fellow developer, a potential collaborator, or simply passing through, 
thank you for taking the time to explore – it is truly appreciated. I hope something here has resonated 
with you, and I wholeheartedly wish you success and fulfilment in your own journey.
