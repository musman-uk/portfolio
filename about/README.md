## 📖 About  

My journey with technology began as curiosity with computers and video games at an early age, and has since grown into a deeper passion for technology as
a whole, where I now work independently creating small games, websites, and tools that reflect my background, experience, 
and values.

I strive to work with integrity by being honest about the process, authenticity by building only what I genuinely believe in, 
empathy by taking accountability for how my work could affect others, and humility by remembering there is always more to learn.

Technology, when used responsibly and kindly, is an invaluable tool for growth, connection, and empowerment. Whether you are a fellow contributor, 
a potential collaborator, or simply passing through, thank you for taking the time to explore – it is truly appreciated, and
I wholeheartedly wish you success and fulfilment in your own journey.
