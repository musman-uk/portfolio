## 📖 About

My journey into technology has been purposeful rather than conventional. From my early years, I was captivated by computers and video games, with countless hours spent exploring them with curiosity and wonder. This first drew me toward video game development, but over time I realised my passion extended to technology as a whole. I often imagined what it might be like to communicate with computers more directly and to be part of the wider world of technology, but felt it was out of reach due to doubts about my abilities.

In order to grow my abilities, I first built a foundation by working in a restaurant, which instilled hard work, resilience, and discipline. Determined to refine myself further, I completed an Access course to Science, went on to earn a degree in Psychology, and gained additional experience in healthcare, events hospitality, and cultural heritage. Though this background strengthens my skillset, it also deepen my understanding of people and systems, guide me to think and act ethically, and help me move beyond those doubts. I now put forth my best efforts into finding my path in technology, with what began as curiosity a deliberate pursuit, and what was once wonder becoming a craft – a reminder that they were not just early sparks, but enduring threads in my work.

This portfolio is now woven into my journey and integral to my current path. It is not a definitive statement or a showcase of perfection, but a living record of learning, persistence, consistency, and dedication, capturing both the progress I have made and the areas that are still evolving. Each project reflects not only technical practice but also a broader engagement with technology, from design and problem solving to communication and intelligent systems, showing how different disciplines and perspectives come together in my work. I endeavour to create a portfolio that is impactful, transparent, and intentional, so that it not only demonstrates what I have built, but also the thinking, influences, and reasoning that shape it.

In terms of the values embedded in this portfolio, I strive to work with, integrity by being honest about the process, authenticity by building only what I genuinely believe in, empathy by taking accountability for how my work affects those who engage with it, and humility by remembering there is always more to learn. These values guide not only my work in this portfolio, but also how my work contributes more broadly and how I hope to work with others. They serve as my lodestar in any situation, be it when facing challenges or making decisions, keeping me aligned with the principles that matter most.

Technology gives us so many ways to grow, connect and allow versatile ideas to travel, evolve, and spark new possibilities. When it is utilized responsibly and kindly, I believe it is an invaluable tool that can encourage and empower us in all of our journeys. So whether you are a fellow contributor, a potential collaborator, someone seeking inspiration, or simply passing through, thank you for taking the time to explore – it truly is appreciated. I hope something here resonates with you, and I wholeheartedly wish you success and fulfilment in your own journey.
