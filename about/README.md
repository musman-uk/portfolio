## 📖 About

My journey into technology has been purposeful rather than conventional. From early curiosity with computers and video games, I grew into a broader passion for technology as a whole. Along the way, I built resilience and perspective through diverse experiences in work and study, which deepened my understanding of people and systems and strengthened my commitment to ethical practice.

This portfolio now extends that journey. It is not a definitive statement or a showcase of perfection, but a living record of learning, persistence, consistency, and dedication, capturing both the progress I have made and the areas that are still evolving. Each project reflects not only technical practice but also a broader engagement with technology, from design and problem solving to communication and intelligent systems, showing how different disciplines and perspectives come together in my work. I endeavour to create a portfolio that is impactful, transparent, and intentional, so that it not only demonstrates what I have built, but also the thinking, influences, and reasoning behind it.

In terms of the values embedded in this portfolio, I strive to work with integrity by being honest about the process, authenticity by building only what I genuinely believe in, empathy by taking accountability for how my work affects those who engage with it, and humility by remembering there is always more to learn. These values guide not only my work in this portfolio, but also how my work contributes more broadly and how I hope to work with others. They serve as my lodestar in any situation, be it when facing challenges or making decisions, keeping me aligned with the principles that matter most.

Technology offers us many ways to grow, connect and help versatile ideas to travel, evolve, and spark new possibilities. When used responsibly and kindly, I believe it is an invaluable tool that can encourage and empower us in all of our journeys. So whether you are a fellow contributor, a potential collaborator, someone seeking inspiration, or simply passing through, thank you for taking the time to explore – it truly is appreciated. I hope something here resonates with you, and I wholeheartedly wish you success and fulfilment in your own journey.
