## 📖 About  

My journey with technology began as curiosity with computers and video games
at an early age. It has since grown into a deeper passion and my craft,
because I believe that when technology is truthful, responsible, and kind,
it can do something good.

To do some good through my craft, I strive to have integrity by being
honest about the process, authenticity by building only what I genuinely
believe in, empathy by being accountable for how my work can affect
others, and humility by remembering there is always more to learn.

So whether you are a fellow developer, a potential collaborator, or
simply passing through, thank you for taking the time to explore – it is
truly appreciated. I hope something here has resonated with you, and I
wholeheartedly wish you success and fulfilment in your own journey.
